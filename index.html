Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MediCare Plus services, appointments and policies

How do I book an appointment at MediCare Plus?

+

You can book through our website calendar, by clicking "Book Now," calling +966 11 000 0000, or using our patient portal. We confirm all bookings within 2 hours with an SMS and email confirmation including your appointment details.

Is 24/7 emergency care truly available?

+

Yes — our Emergency Department never closes. We have 80+ emergency beds, a dedicated trauma center, helicopter landing pad, and a full team of emergency physicians, surgeons and nurses on duty every hour of every day. For life-threatening emergencies, call 911 immediately.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

+

We accept all major Saudi health insurance plans including Bupa Arabia, Tawuniya, MedGulf, AXA Cooperative, Al Rajhi Takaful and all CCHI-approved insurers. We also offer direct billing — no need to pay upfront. Please bring your insurance card to every visit.

How does teleconsultation work?

+

Book a teleconsult appointment online, choose your specialist, pay securely (SAR 150 per session) and join via the link sent to your email — no app required. After the consultation, receive your prescription and medical report digitally within minutes. Available 24/7.

How quickly can I get lab results?

+

Routine blood tests: 2-4 hours. Urgent tests: within 60 minutes. Specialized tests: 24-48 hours. All results are automatically uploaded to your patient portal with instant notification. For abnormal results, our team will call you directly.

What are your visiting hours for admitted patients?

+

General wards: 1PM-3PM and 6PM-9PM daily. ICU/CCU: 2PM-4PM (2 visitors max). Pediatric ward: parents allowed 24/7. Please note visiting may be restricted during rounds or isolation protocols. Confirm with the nursing station before visiting.
